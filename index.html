About

We are a team of aspiring web developers with a creative flare and a passion to learn and grow

Between the two of us we cover both sides of the build: clean, accessible markup and styling on the front, and the logic that makes it work underneath. Every project is a chance to sharpen our craft, so we treat small builds with the same care as big ones — readable code, thoughtful design, and pages that feel good to use on any screen.

We're early in the journey, curious by default, and always shipping something new.

Team
